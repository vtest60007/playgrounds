## 🧩 Playground Scenarios & Requirements

### 1. **Form Validation Playground (`index.html`)**
- Age field must only accept values between 18 and 60.  
- Email must contain “@” and a valid domain.  
- Password must be at least 6 characters and include a number.  
- Payment + Shipping combinations must follow rules (e.g., COD not allowed with Express).  
- Order states must follow valid progression (New → Processing → Shipped → Delivered).  


---

### 2. **Shopping Cart Checkout (`shopping.html`)**
- Quantity must be between 1 and 10.  
- Coupon codes must begin with “SAVE”.  
- Checkout requires both payment and delivery selections.  
- Certain payment + delivery combinations are invalid (e.g., COD + Same Day).  
- Cart flow must follow proper sequence (Cart → Checkout → Payment → Confirmation).  
  

---

### 3. **Login & Role-Based Access (`login.html`)**
- Username must be 5–12 characters.  
- Password must be 6–10 characters and include at least one digit.  
- Admin role requires special password format (`@admin`).  
- User role requires numeric password.  
- Login/logout must follow valid sequence.  


---

### 4. **Flight Booking (`flight.html`)**
- Passenger count must be between 1 and 5.  
- Departure and arrival cities must be different.  
- Travel date cannot be in the past.  
- Certain seat class + payment combinations are invalid (e.g., Business + COD).  
- Booking flow must follow proper sequence (Search → Select → Payment → Confirmation).  


---

### 5. **Banking Transaction (`banking.html`)**
- Withdrawal amount must be between 100 and 10,000.  
- Transaction type must be Deposit or Withdrawal.  
- Savings account withdrawals cannot exceed 5000.  
- Account state must follow proper lifecycle (Active → Transaction → Updated Balance → Closed).  


---

### 6. **Healthcare Appointment (`healthcare.html`)**
- Patient age must be between 1 and 120.  
- Appointment type must be General, Specialist, or Emergency.  
- Appointment date cannot be in the past.  
- Certain appointment type + time slot combinations are invalid (e.g., General + Night).  
- Appointment lifecycle must follow proper sequence (Registered → Scheduled → Checked In → Completed).  
 

---

### 7. **Exam Registration (`exam.html`)**
- Student age must be between 10 and 25.  
- Subject must be Math, Science, or English.  
- Exam date cannot be in the past.  
- Certain subject + exam type combinations are invalid (e.g., Science must be Offline).  
- Exam lifecycle must follow proper sequence (Registered → Payment → Admit Card → Exam Taken).  


---

### 8. **Hotel Booking (`hotel.html`)**
- Nights must be between 1 and 30.  
- Room type must be Standard, Deluxe, or Suite.  
- Check-in date cannot be in the past.  
- Certain room type + payment combinations are invalid (e.g., Suite + COD).  
- Booking lifecycle must follow proper sequence (Reserved → Checked In → Checked Out → Completed).  


---

### 9. **Library Borrowing (`library.html`)**
- Borrow duration must be between 1 and 30 days.  
- Book category must be Fiction, Non-Fiction, or Reference.  
- Borrow date cannot be in the past.  
- Certain category + duration combinations are invalid (e.g., Reference ≤ 7 days).  
- Borrow lifecycle must follow proper sequence (Borrowed → Returned → Overdue → Closed).  
 

---

### 10. **Restaurant Reservation (`restaurant.html`)**
- Guest count must be between 1 and 20.  
- Reservation type must be Lunch, Dinner, or Private Event.  
- Reservation date cannot be in the past.  
- Certain reservation type + payment combinations are invalid (e.g., Event + COD).  
- Reservation lifecycle must follow proper sequence (Reserved → Seated → Served → Completed).  


---

### 11. **Transport Ticket Booking (`transport.html`)**
- Passenger count must be between 1 and 6.  
- Route type must be Local, Express, or Interstate.  
- Travel date cannot be in the past.  
- Certain route type + payment combinations are invalid (e.g., Express + COD).  
- Ticket lifecycle must follow proper sequence (Booked → Payment → Confirmed → Boarded → Completed).  


---
